# Create supplier (request)

**Request type:** `create_supplier` · **Target:** 8 h (net working time)

## Who
| Raised by | Prepared by | Approved by |
|---|---|---|
| Operations or any budget holder | Steward | Head of Operations |

The approver is never the person who raised the request.

## Evidence required
- business reason
- internal contact

## Checks before approval
Quality rules: C01, V03, U01 (if a tax ID is given). A request with a failed error-level rule or missing evidence
cannot be submitted.
