# Erase a contact person

**Request type:** `erase_contact` · **Target:** 40 h, and never more than one calendar month (net working time)

## Who
| Raised by | Prepared by | Approved by |
|---|---|---|
| Steward, on the DPO's instruction, with the DPO reference | Steward | DPO |

The approver is never the person who raised the request, so the DPO does not raise it: the DPO receives the data subject's request and instructs the steward. The one-calendar-month limit runs from the day the DPO received it.

## Evidence required
- dpo reference

## Checks before approval
Quality rules: None (the record is removed from every source; proof is kept without the erased data). A request with a failed error-level rule or missing evidence
cannot be submitted.
