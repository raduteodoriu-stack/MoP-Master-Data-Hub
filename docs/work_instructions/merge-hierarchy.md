# Merge, undo merge, hierarchy change

**Request type:** `merge_hierarchy` · **Target:** 16 h (net working time)

## Who
| Raised by | Prepared by | Approved by |
|---|---|---|
| System or any requester (merge); Sales or steward (hierarchy) | Steward | Steward for a probable merge; data owner to undo a merge or when owner, legal or bank data would change; Sales Director for hierarchy |

The approver is never the person who raised the request.

## Evidence required
- reason
- records concerned

## Checks before approval
Quality rules: U01, U02, K05. A request with a failed error-level rule or missing evidence
cannot be submitted.
