# Reactivate a dormant supplier

**Request type:** `reactivate_supplier` · **Target:** 16 h (net working time)

## Who
| Raised by | Prepared by | Approved by |
|---|---|---|
| Operations or any budget holder | Steward, after verifying the bank details again | Finance Controller |

The approver is never the person who raised the request.

## Evidence required
- business reason
- bank re-verification (through an active contact verified in the last 12 months, K12)

## Checks before approval
Quality rules: T02, K10, K11, K12, V13. A dormant supplier is a common fraud target, so its bank details are always verified again before it can be paid.
