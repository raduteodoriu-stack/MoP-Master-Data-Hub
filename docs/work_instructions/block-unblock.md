# Block or unblock

**Request type:** `block_unblock` · **Target:** 4 h (net working time)

## Who
| Raised by | Prepared by | Approved by |
|---|---|---|
| Finance, Sales or Operations | Steward | Finance Controller for credit, payment or compliance; Sales Director or Head of Operations for inactive |

The approver is never the person who raised the request.

## Evidence required
- reason

## Checks before approval
Quality rules: K07. A request with a failed error-level rule or missing evidence
cannot be submitted.
