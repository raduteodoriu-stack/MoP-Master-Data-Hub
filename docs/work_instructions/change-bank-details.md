# Change supplier bank details

**Request type:** `change_bank_details` · **Target:** 16 h (net working time)

## Who
| Raised by | Prepared by | Approved by |
|---|---|---|
| Finance (payables) | Steward, after independent verification with the supplier | Finance Controller |

The approver is never the person who raised the request.

## Evidence required
- bank certificate
- independent verification via contact on file

## Checks before approval
Quality rules: V09, K02, K03, U03, T02. A request with a failed error-level rule or missing evidence
cannot be submitted.
