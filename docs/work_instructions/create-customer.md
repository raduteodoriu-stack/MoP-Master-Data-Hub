# Create customer (promote a prospect)

**Request type:** `create_customer` · **Target:** 16 h (net working time)

## Who
| Raised by | Prepared by | Approved by |
|---|---|---|
| Sales or Finance | Steward | Finance Controller |

The approver is never the person who raised the request.

## Evidence required
- signed contract reference
- tax registration

## Checks before approval
Quality rules: C01, V01, V02, V04, V06, V08, K01, K04, U01. A request with a failed error-level rule or missing evidence
cannot be submitted.
