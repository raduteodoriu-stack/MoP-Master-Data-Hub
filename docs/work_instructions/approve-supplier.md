# Approve supplier for payment

**Request type:** `approve_supplier` · **Target:** 16 h (net working time)

## Who
| Raised by | Prepared by | Approved by |
|---|---|---|
| Operations | Steward (checks tax ID and bank evidence) | Finance Controller |

The approver is never the person who raised the request.

## Evidence required
- tax registration
- bank certificate
- verification record

## Checks before approval
Quality rules: C01, V01, V02, V09, K01, K02, K03, U01, U03, T02. A request with a failed error-level rule or missing evidence
cannot be submitted.
